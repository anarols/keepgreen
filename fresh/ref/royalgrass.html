Our grass innovations

Thanks to our technologies and the use of high-quality materials, we have developed artificial grass that not only replicates the visual characteristics of natural grass but also the functional properties of natural grass. Discover it here.

V-Shape®

The patented V-Shape and strengthening spine of the grass blade provide more resilience, allowing the grass to stay upright better and longer.

Learn more about the V-Shape.

MiNT®

Microscopic veins on the blade of grass provide a mattifying effect, resulting in a natural shine.

ReaDY®

Just like natural grass, every blade of grass has its own ‘growth direction’. The blades of grass face in different directions, which makes our artificial grass come to life.

Read more about the ReaDY technology.

SunTEC®

Here and there you will find a higher sun-tanned blade of grass between the grass, which provides a natural summer look.

REBel® backing

Our REBel backing consists of the same raw materials as our artificial grass fibers, which makes our artificial grass 100% circular. In addition, the REBel backing is lighter in weight and ensures minimal stretch and shrinkage.
