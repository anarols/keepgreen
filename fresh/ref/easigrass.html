Easigrass The UK’s Most Awarded Artificial Grass & Fake Lawn Supplier and Installer

The UK’s premier artificial grass supplier and installer, Easigrass delivers quality products backed by more than three decades of experience transforming outdoor and indoor spaces. As a multi-award-winning brand, we are trusted for our expertise, innovation, and exceptional results. Ultra-realistic in look and feel, our unrivalled range of British designed floral and artificial grass products deliver stylish and practical design solutions to suit a variety of specific applications, including pet friendly artificial grass, artificial grass for schools, fake grass for gardens, commercial artificial grass, and a range of other domestic and commercial applications.

We are proud of the outstanding reputation for quality in design and development we have built over 30 years. We work only with the highest grades of materials and latest yarn technology to create natural-looking, versatile and durable artificial grass installations.
